## Music you make with it

**Music and sound effects made with this software may be used freely, whether
commercially or not.** There are no fees. The copyright in what you make is
yours.

You do not need to report or credit anything, but the author would be glad to
[hear from you](#contact).

## The software itself

The distributed plugins (VST3) are covered by the **GPLv3**. They link against
the "JUCE" framework and the "VST3 SDK", and inherit their licences (GPLv3 /
AGPLv3).

**The source code in the repository is under the MIT License.** The compiled
binary, however, follows the GPLv3 as above, so distributing it means meeting
the GPLv3's terms.

### What is not allowed

- Redistributing it while misrepresenting its authorship
- Selling it as a commercial product without the author's permission

Under the GPLv3, altering or removing the copyright notice is not permitted.

The presets in the `OPLL Roms` folder are based on the patch data in
[Copyright free OPLL(x) ROM patches](https://github.com/plgDavid/misc/wiki/Copyright-free-OPLL(x)-ROM-patches),
published by David of Plogue.

The original data is published under `CC BY-SA`, so these presets carry the same
terms.
